An agent plans, retrieves context, uses tools, remembers state, and reports a result. Each step brings ordinary engineering constraints: latency, shared state, permissions, cost, failures, and ownership. These are twenty rules worth knowing before you design that system.

Every article connects one rule to a worked agent scenario, an illustrated workflow, and a “Before you build” decision. The scenarios are hypothetical design examples. The series distinguishes mathematical results from models, observations, and humorous warnings; these ideas were not all invented for AI.
