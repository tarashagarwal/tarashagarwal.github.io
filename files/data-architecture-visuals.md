Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: wide editorial cover illustration for a technical blog article about modern data architecture and AI. Primary request: depict an elegant miniature abstract data city on a dark navy background: structured glass data tables at left; three ascending bronze, silver, gold translucent terraces in the middle; a delicate connected knowledge graph of luminous nodes and edges flowing upward; two distinct batch and streaming light paths; a large luminous teacher-model structure guiding a smaller efficient student-model structure at right. Style: sophisticated isometric 3D editorial illustration, restrained sci-fi, physically plausible frosted glass and brushed metal, crisp forms, refined composition, generous negative space, not a busy infographic. Palette: navy, cyan, muted violet, bronze, silver, subtle gold. Composition: landscape approximately 16:9, visual objects centered with breathing room, balanced bright accents and dark negative space. No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no watermarks. Treat the illustration as conceptual artwork, not a factual labeled architecture diagram.
